### MARITIME DISCOVERY:

**Rudder Repair:**
- Structural part that hangs under boat, faces constant damage
- Sailing team gets chips in rudders all the time
- Current process: fiberglass layer → sand → paint (repeat often)
- Carbon fiber is 3x stronger than fiberglass
- Opportunity: Longer-lasting repairs on high-stress areas

**Rigging:**
- Wires and metal connections keep mast upright
- Metal exposed to salt → salt eats everything, even stainless steel
- When those break, that's when you need welding on the water
- But boatyard guy does mostly fiberglass repairs

**Multi-Day Races (Insurance Angle):**
- San Diego to Catalina Island: Friday-Sunday races
- If something breaks mid-race → towboat rescue
- Boatyard guy: "These people, if something breaks out there, they usually have to send a towboat"

**Composites Mentor Connection:**
- Boatyard guy connected Juan to mentor who's "really big into composites"
- Meeting next Wednesday
- Could help on technical side
