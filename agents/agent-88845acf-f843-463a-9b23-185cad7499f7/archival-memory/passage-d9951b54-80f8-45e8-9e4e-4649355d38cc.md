**PROCESSING FRAMEWORK: Neal's Session Structure Pattern (Feb 6-7 sessions)**

Every session follows this structure:
1. **Listen first** — let the founder surface where they actually are
2. **Reframe** — Craig or Neal drops a framework/analogy that shifts how the founder sees the problem (Tiger Woods model, 2x2 matrix, wraparound concept, AI Audit team). THESE ARE THE HIGH-SIGNAL MOMENTS. Flag any before/after shift in how the founder understood their own problem.
3. **Debate openly** — mentors sometimes disagree in front of founders (whale vs volume for Billy, tape vs welding for Juan). This is intentional, not dysfunction. Capture both sides.
4. **End with behavioral deliverable** — not "think about X" but "do Y by date Z and report what you learned"

**CRITICAL NUANCE — Commitment Ownership:**
Neal's philosophy: founders OPT INTO their own timelines. When extracting commitments, always note whether:
- The commitment came FROM the founder (self-initiated — higher signal)
- The commitment was ASSIGNED by a mentor (externally imposed — may not stick)
This distinction matters to Neal. He explicitly corrected his own agent for imposing a deadline on Billy.
