### KEY DISCOVERY: PITCH REFRAME NEEDED

**The Problem:**
- Zach starts conversations with "Do you use AI in your practice?"
- This makes lawyers defensive — they say "no" or "I don't need this"
- Solo practitioners say "I trust myself, this is for associates"
- Craig + Neal don't believe them: "They're sending sensitive data"

**The Gas Station Junk Food Problem (Craig's insight):**
- Somehow when you buy junk food at a gas station, it doesn't count against your diet
- Same with lawyers using AI — somehow it "doesn't count" as violating confidentiality
- They're in denial about their own behavior

**The Reframe:**
- DON'T start with "Do you use AI?"
- DO start with: "What stops you from putting personal information in the wrong places?"
- It's not about AI — it's about sensitive data EVERYWHERE (email, PDFs, cloud uploads)
- Craig: "I barely know my own Social Security number. I'm not putting someone else's in Claude."

---

### DEMO VALIDATED (First Time Craig Saw It):

**What the tool does:**
- Browser extension (like Grammarly widget)
- Shows yellow/red warnings as you type sensitive data
- Picks up 60+ types: SSN, email, phone, financial data, client names, government IDs
- Can tokenize/obfuscate sensitive data with one click
- Runs completely local (no cloud transmission)
- Dashboard shows all flagged items, can export compliance report

**Craig's reaction:**
- "I look at it and I go, of course I want this on my shoulder. 100%."
- "This is one of the more compelling demos I've seen."
- "I'm shocked... I've seen a lot of much shittier demos get traction."

**Why lawyers might not see value:**
- They think cloud encryption = security (confusing transmission security with data loss prevention)
- They're in denial about their own behavior
- Solo practitioners don't want to monitor themselves
- Zach's pitch triggers defensiveness

### THE COLLISON INSTALLATION (Neal's Teaching):

**What it is:**
- Patrick Collison (Stripe founder) sat with first 200 customers personally
- "Let me come to your office, let me sit next to you, and let me integrate you with our service"
- "I will not leave your side until I get you up and running"
- If Patrick Collison can do that (now $100-200B company), Zach can do that

**The application:**
- Don't wait for Chrome Web Store approval
- Don't send lawyers to landing page
- GO TO THEIR OFFICE and install it yourself
- Walk through the beta warnings
- Stay until they're using it

**The test:**
- After 30 days: "If I were to shut down the service, would you be upset?"
- If they say "it was fun but I'm okay" → not enough value
- If they say "NO DON'T TAKE IT AWAY" → you have product-market fit
