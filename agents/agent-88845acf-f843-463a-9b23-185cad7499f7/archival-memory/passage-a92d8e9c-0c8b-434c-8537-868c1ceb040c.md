## Think Tank Team Session - March 26, 2026

**Meeting Type:** 1:1 with Craig (Live In The Future track)
**Team:** Dimitri, Jailyn, + others
**Product:** Sports recruiting platform (soccer-focused) - AI-powered player profiles and coach matching
**Duration:** ~45 minutes

---

### PRODUCT EVOLUTION

**Starting Point:** Skill development app (Strava for soccer)
**Current Direction:** "LinkedIn for sports" - recruiting + social platform

**Why the shift:**
- AI will make skill feedback easy and free (upload to ChatGPT)
- Hard part becomes: Where do I share? How do I get discovered?
- Network effect opportunity: Player inventory + coach search

---

### PRODUCT FEATURES (MVP)

**Player Side:**
- Upload highlight clips
- AI-generated player profile with attributes (speed, vision, strength, endurance)
- Skills breakdown (strengths, weaknesses, tendencies)
- "Best fit system" recommendation
- Social feed (view/comment on other players' clips)
- FIFA-style attribute visualization ⭐ SIGNATURE FEATURE

**Coach Side:**
- AI prompt search for specific player types
- Qualitative search: "creative playmaker with exceptional vision, both feet"
- Match scores for player-coach system fit
- View highlights and profiles

### STRATEGIC DIRECTION

**Craig's guidance:**
1. **Focus on single-player experience first** - Enough value for a player to upload independent of coaches
2. **Don't validate with paying customers yet** - This is frontier territory
3. **Get the "wow" reaction** - Show to 10 real players, get "I would definitely sign up"
4. **Build inventory before coach side** - Players first, coaches later

**Key question:** Is there enough value for a single player to upload and maintain their profile without any coaches?

**Answer:** Yes - the attribute visualization is compelling on its own

---

### TECHNICAL APPROACH

**Current:** Built mockup in Lovable
**Recommendations:**
- **Gemini** for video analysis (best at multimodal/video - has YouTube training data)
- **Google AI Studio** for video-focused development
- **Computer vision** for player tracking (Craig shared tweet with example)
- **Cursor** for more powerful development (SDSU has free Pro version)

**Workflow:**
- Can manually do analysis in background for MVP
- Doesn't need to be fully automated yet
- Focus on getting emotional reaction first
