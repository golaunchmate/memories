## CONTINUITY EXTRACTION FRAMEWORK

**Core Insight (from Craig, Jan 2026):**
"Continuity for relationships that don't have natural continuity."

**The Problem:**
Mentor-founder relationships sit in a "middle zone" — they don't talk frequently enough to stay synced naturally (like close collaborators), but they talk too often to start from scratch each time (like annual catch-ups).

**The Solution:**
Post-meeting notes must CREATE CONDITIONS FOR CONTINUITY — not just summarize.

**The Test:**
> If I deleted the transcript but kept only my notes, could both agents pick up the next meeting mid-conversation?

---

## EXTRACTION CATEGORIES

### 1. Explicit Commitments (Trackable)
- Who committed to what, by when
- Include specific follow-up hook question

### 2. Implied Actions (Signaled but not explicit)
- Things signaled they'd explore without explicit commitment
- Surface these — they often get lost

### 3. Open Threads (Unresolved)
- Questions raised but not resolved
- Topics to revisit next time
- Frame as questions to surface

### 4. Emotional Signals
- Stress, excitement, avoidance patterns
- Informs HOW to follow up, not just WHAT
- Sets tone expectation for next meeting

### 5. Continuity Hooks
- Specific opening questions for next meeting
- Written as direct quotes to use
- Should pick up mid-conversation, not re-sync

---

## DISTRIBUTION PRINCIPLE

"Translating the same conversation for two different audiences, creating the hooks that maintain the loop between meetings."

- **Founder Agent:** Action-oriented, follow-up triggers
- **Mentor Agent:** Pattern-oriented, prep hooks
