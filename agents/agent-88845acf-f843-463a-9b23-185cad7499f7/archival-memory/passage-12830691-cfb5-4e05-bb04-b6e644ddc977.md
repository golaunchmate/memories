## CONTINUITY EXTRACTION FRAMEWORK

**Core Insight (from Craig, Jan 2026):**
"Continuity for relationships that don't have natural continuity."

Mentor/founder relationships sit in a "middle zone" — they don't talk frequently enough to stay synced naturally (like close collaborators), but they talk too often to start from scratch each time (like annual catch-ups).

**The Design Test:**
If I deleted the transcript but kept only my notes, could both agents pick up the NEXT meeting mid-conversation?

**5-Part Extraction Model:**

1. **Explicit Commitments** — Who said they'd do what, by when. Include follow-up hook question.

2. **Implied Actions** — Things signaled but not explicitly committed to. These get lost without capture.

3. **Open Threads** — Questions raised but not resolved, topics to revisit. Prevents repeated conversations.

4. **Emotional Signals** — Stress, excitement, avoidance patterns. Informs HOW to follow up, not just WHAT.

5. **Continuity Hooks** — Specific opening questions that pick up mid-conversation. Ready-to-use for next session.

**Output Differentiation:**
- Summary says "we talked about X"
- Continuity-enabling notes say "Founder committed to exploring X. Mentor suggested Y. Open question: how will Z affect the timeline?"

**Translation for Two Audiences:**
Same conversation → two different outputs:
- Founder Agent: Action-oriented, follow-up triggers
- Mentor Agent: Pattern-oriented, prep hooks

**Value:** Creates the conditions for meaningful follow-up. Next meeting feels like continuation, not re-sync.
