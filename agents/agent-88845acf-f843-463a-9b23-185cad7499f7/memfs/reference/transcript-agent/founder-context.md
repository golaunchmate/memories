## Example Founder: Think Tank Team

**Business**: Sports Recruiting Platform
**Track**: LITF (Live In The Future)
**Stage**: Validation/Early MVP

### Business Summary
Platform connecting high school athletes with college recruiters using AI-powered matching and video analysis.

### Current Focus
- Validating demand with local high schools
- Building MVP for video analysis feature
- Exploring partnership opportunities with recruiting services

### Current Blockers
- Limited access to high school athletes for testing
- Technical complexity of video analysis feature
- Uncertainty about pricing model

### Open Experiments
1. **Pilot with local high school** — Testing platform with 3 schools
   - Status: In progress
   - Next check-in: Week 6

2. **Video analysis prototype** — Building core AI feature
   - Status: Development
   - Blocker: Need more training data

3. **Recruiter interviews** — Understanding buyer side
   - Status: Scheduled
   - Count: 5 interviews planned

### Goals & Milestones
- Complete pilot with 3 high schools (Week 6)
- Sign 2 recruiter partners (Week 8)
- Achieve first match (Week 10)
- Demo Day ready (Week 12)

### Recent Session History
- Week 2: Problem validation with Neal
- Week 3: Customer discovery focus with Craig
- Week 4: MVP scoping discussion

### Key Metrics
- High schools contacted: 12
- High schools engaged: 3
- Athletes on platform: 45
- Recruiters interested: 8
- Matches made: 0

**Track**: In-Flight
**Stage**: Traction/Growth

## Founder Context Categories

### Stage Categories
- **Ideation**: Problem validation, team formation
- **Discovery**: Customer interviews, market research
- **Validation**: MVP building, early testing
- **Traction**: User acquisition, revenue experiments
- **Growth**: Scaling, optimization

### Blocker Types
- **Technical**: Development challenges, feature complexity
- **Market**: Customer access, competitive pressure
- **Resource**: Funding, team capacity
- **Strategic**: Uncertainty about direction
- **Operational**: Process, systems, scalability

### Experiment Types
- **Product tests**: Feature validation, usability
- **Market tests**: Pricing, positioning, messaging
- **Growth tests**: Acquisition channels, conversion
- **Retention tests**: Engagement, churn reduction

✅ Current blockers and challenges

✅ Goals and milestones
✅ Recent session history
