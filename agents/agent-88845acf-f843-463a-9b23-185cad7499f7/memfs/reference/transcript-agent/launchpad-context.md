# ZIP Launchpad Program Context

## Program Overview

ZIP Launchpad is San Diego State University's startup incubator program that helps student founders turn ideas into viable businesses through mentorship, resources, and structured milestones.

---

## Program Timeline (Spring 2026 Example)

| Week | Phase | Focus | Key Activities |
|------|-------|-------|----------------|
| 1-2 | Launch | Team formation, ideation | Team building, problem validation |
| 3-4 | Discovery | Customer discovery | Interviews, market research |
| 5-6 | Validation | MVP development | Build-test-learn cycles |
| 7-8 | Traction | Early metrics | User acquisition, revenue experiments |
| 9-10 | Growth | Scaling strategies | Growth hacks, partnerships |
| 11-12 | Demo Day Prep | Pitch refinement | Storytelling, deck creation |
| 13 | Demo Day | Final presentations | Investor showcase |

---

## Key Milestones

### Milestone 1: Problem Validation (Week 2)
- Clear problem statement
- Target customer identified
- Initial hypothesis documented

### Milestone 2: Customer Discovery (Week 4)
- 20+ customer interviews completed
- Patterns identified in customer pain points
- Pivot or persevere decision made

### Milestone 3: MVP Definition (Week 6)
- Minimum viable product spec
- Core features identified
- Build timeline established

### Milestone 4: Traction (Week 8)
- Working MVP deployed
- First users acquired
- Initial feedback collected

### Milestone 5: Demo Day Ready (Week 12)
- Pitch deck complete
- Story refined
- Metrics documented
- Growth trajectory clear

## LITF (Live In The Future) Track

Some founders participate in the LITF (Live In The Future) track, which focuses on:
- Emerging technologies
- Future-forward business models
- Cutting-edge applications
- Experimental approaches

**Recognition**: LITF founders may discuss futuristic concepts, AI applications, or novel business models.

---

## Demo Day

**Purpose**: Showcase startups to investors, mentors, and community

**Format**:
- 5-7 minute pitches
- Q&A with judges
- Networking session
- Awards ceremony

**Preparation**:
- Pitch deck refinement
- Storytelling practice
- Metrics documentation
- Demo preparation

**Recognition**: Late-stage sessions focus heavily on pitch refinement, story arc, and investor-readiness.

---

## In-Flight Cohort

The In-Flight cohort represents founders who are actively building and testing their ventures. These founders:
- Have validated problems
- Are in build/test cycles
- May have early users
- Are refining their approach

**Recognition**: In-Flight founders discuss experiments, metrics, blockers, and iteration rather than ideation.
