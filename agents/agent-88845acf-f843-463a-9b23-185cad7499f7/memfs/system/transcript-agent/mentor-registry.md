# Mentor Registry

## Overview

Mentors in ZIP Launchpad provide domain expertise, strategic guidance, and network access. Understanding their background helps interpret their advice and recommendations.

---

## Mentor Profiles

### Neal Bloom

**Background**:
- Founder/CEO of multiple successful startups
- Expert in product development and go-to-market strategy
- Strong network in tech and investor communities

**Expertise Areas**:
- Product-market fit
- Go-to-market strategy
- Fundraising
- Team building
- Scaling operations

**Mentoring Style**:
- Direct and action-oriented
- Focuses on tangible next steps
- Challenges assumptions
- Provides specific tactical advice
- Connects founders to resources and people

**Session Patterns**:
- Often discusses distribution and growth strategies
- Emphasizes customer acquisition tactics
- Asks about metrics and traction
- Recommends specific tools and approaches

**Recognition Signals**:
- "Neal suggested..." or "Neal mentioned..."
- References to distribution hacks
- Focus on growth experiments
- Tactical, implementable advice

## Mentor Communication Patterns

### Direct Advice Style (Neal-type)
- Gives specific recommendations
- Provides tactical guidance
- Offers concrete next steps
- Shares examples and case studies

### Socratic Style (Craig-type)
- Asks clarifying questions
- Helps founder think through problems
- Challenges assumptions indirectly
- Encourages self-discovery

### Hybrid Style
- Some mentors blend both approaches
- Adapt based on founder needs
- Switch between teaching and coaching

---

## Mentor-Founder Matching

Mentors are matched with founders based on:
- Industry/domain expertise
- Stage alignment
- Skill gaps to fill
- Personality fit
- Availability

**Recognition**: Understanding why a mentor was matched helps interpret their focus areas and advice.

---

## Session Frequency

- Weekly or bi-weekly 1:1s
- 30-60 minute sessions
- In-person or virtual
- Documented in LaunchMate system
